AeroPulse Studios Company Policy

1. Respect and Professionalism

All staff and community members are expected to treat each other with respect. Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or targeted abuse will not be tolerated.

2. Confidentiality

Internal project discussions, assets, and tools are confidential. Do not share unreleased content, source material, or internal conversations outside of approved channels.

3. Fair Moderation

Moderators must enforce rules consistently and fairly. Decisions should be based on evidence and server guidelines, not personal relationships or bias.

4. Activity and Communication

Staff should remain reasonably active and communicate absences. Repeated unannounced inactivity may result in removal from the team.

5. Conflict of Interest

Staff must disclose conflicts of interest and recuse themselves from decisions involving friends or associates when impartiality cannot be maintained.

6. Reporting and Appeals

Users may appeal moderation actions through designated channels. Staff are required to review appeals objectively and escalate when uncertain.

7. Amendment

These policies may be updated at any time. Continued participation in AeroPulse Studios communities or projects constitutes agreement to the latest version.
