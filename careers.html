QA Tester

As a QA Tester, you'll help us make sure our games are fun, polished, and ready for players. You'll playtest new and updated features, look for bugs and issues, test different systems and mechanics, and make sure everything works as intended.

  • Playtest games across different stages of development
  • Test new features, updates, gameplay systems, and mechanics
  • Find and report bugs, glitches, and unexpected issues
  • Provide clear and constructive feedback to the development team
  • Reproduce bugs and provide detailed steps to help developers fix them
Remote Full-time
Hot position

QA Testing Lead

As a QA Testing Lead, you'll guide the QA team's testing efforts, define test plans, and ensure we ship stable, polished releases. You'll coordinate with developers and managers to prioritize issues and improve our QA pipeline.

  • Lead and coordinate the QA team's testing efforts
  • Create and maintain test plans, checklists, and bug-tracking workflows
  • Prioritize issues and communicate them clearly to developers
  • Oversee testing across devices and builds
  • Drive improvements to the QA pipeline and release quality
Remote Full-time
Hiring

Moderation Officer

As a Moderation Officer, you will help maintain a safe, welcoming, and professional environment across AeroPulse Studios. Moderation Officers are responsible for monitoring communities, responding to reports, enforcing rules fairly, assisting members, and handling situations professionally. Officers are expected to remain active, respectful, unbiased, and reliable while representing AeroPulse Studios.

  • Monitor community spaces and respond to reports
  • Enforce rules fairly, consistently, and without bias
  • Assist members and de-escalate conflicts
  • Document actions and communicate with leadership
  • Represent AeroPulse Studios professionally at all times
Remote Full-time
